About Sumit Ubale — Wedding Photographer in Shrigonda

Based in Shrigonda, Ahilyanagar district, I've spent over 10 years specializing in candid wedding photography and cinematic films across Maharashtra. With 100+ weddings covered — from intimate ceremonies in Shrigonda's heritage venues to grand celebrations in Pune and Mumbai — I bring deep local expertise and an artistic eye to every celebration.

My approach is rooted in observation: capturing authentic, unscripted emotions rather than posed portraits. Whether it's the Haldi rituals, the Varmala exchange, or the Sangeet celebrations — every moment is preserved with cinematic precision. Packages start from ₹25,000 and are fully customized to your needs.

Trusted by families across Shrigonda, Karjat, Jamkhed, Parner, Rahuri, Sangamner, Ahilyanagar, Pune, Mumbai, and Nashik — I'm committed to making your wedding memories timeless.

Services

  • Cinematic Wedding Films – High-production visual storytelling.
  • Pre-Wedding Shoots – Creative sessions at scenic locations.
